# 1. Automation Brief — Aangan Studio phone enquiries

Evidence key: **[Case]** = stated in the case. **[T##/W##/F##]** = the September transcript pack. **[Assumed]** = not in the case; my assumption.

## The Why

**Pain.** Enquiries die before a designer ever sees them. [Case] ~200 enquiries/month, about a third arrive outside the 10am–7pm front-desk window, and ~48% get no reply within 48 hours. [Case] An enquiry answered within 1 hour converts at ~4x the rate of one answered the next day. The transcripts show the mechanism:
- Phone: T08 is a 10:47pm missed call with no voicemail; the call-back the next morning went unanswered. T16: "I called Monday… it's been two days" — the desk had no note of the first call.
- WhatsApp: W01 (7:14pm), W05 (Saturday 6:44pm), W09 and W02 were all sent when the desk was closed. W05 and W02 were lost; W08 ("No one replied in 4 days") was lost-in-the-making and flagged highly frustrated.
- Of the 10 WhatsApp threads, 5 contain an explicit "[No reply]" gap or a multi-day delay (W01, W02, W05, W08, W09).

A second pain is hidden inside the leads that *do* arrive: [Case] they reach a designer as a forwarded message with no context, so the designer's first call re-asks what the desk already asked. In T05, T06, T12, T15 and T20 the desk collected the area, timeline and ownership but the case says none of it travels with the forward.

**User.** Two users, and they want different things.
1. *Nikhil Deshpande, founder* [Case] — wants every enquiry answered in 5 minutes, only worthwhile leads in front of his designers, and he pulled the monthly numbers by hand.
2. *The designer* (14 of them) [Case] — the person who actually receives the lead and must make the first call; wants it pre-asked.
The caller (a homeowner/small-business owner) is the beneficiary, not the buyer.

**Outcome.** Defined so it can be measured:
- Every call is answered at once, at any hour (first-response seconds, not hours).
- Every qualified call reaches a designer as one structured message; the designer never re-asks what is already in it.
- Every unqualified call is closed kindly with a reason, and no price is ever quoted.
- Nikhil sees volume, response time, qualified vs not, handoffs and the system's own cost vs pipeline without pulling anything by hand.

**What it is not.** It does not replace the consultation, quote, or promise anything the studio does not do (see Nine Checks, the Cut).
